Mission Statement

Make Oklahoma's technology safer. We scan for vulnerable devices, share findings with organizations, and help provide advice or technical assistance to improve security.

Welcome to Sooner Secure

Sooner Secure is an Oklahoma-based group that scans the internet to find vulnerable devices and notify organizations before threats can be exploited.

We do not charge for these services. Our outreach, guidance, and technical assistance are provided at no cost.

We reach out to companies and institutions across Oklahoma with notice, advice, and technical support when needed. Our goal is to improve cybersecurity for local businesses, nonprofits, and public institutions.

Working together, we help make Oklahoma’s digital landscape safer, more resilient, and better protected against cyber risks.

We do not access, interact with, or manipulate exposed systems we find online. Any information we collect is publically available, unless shared with us by the owner of the system. We operate in line with local and federal laws. We do not share information about systems to any party besides the operator of the systems.

How We Work

Sooner Secure is in the process of aquiring formal nonprofit status. We do not take payment for assistance provided.

What we do

  • Scan the public internet for exposed or vulnerable devices that may pose a risk.
  • Identify affected organizations and verify the findings with care.
  • Reach out with clear notice, recommended security steps, and resources.

How we help

  • Provide free advice for fixing common exposure issues.
  • Offer technical assistance when organizations need hands-on support.
  • Prioritize respectful communication and responsible disclosure.

Why it matters

By sharing guidance and support at no charge, we reduce risk across Oklahoma’s technology landscape and help organizations stay safer against cyber threats.
